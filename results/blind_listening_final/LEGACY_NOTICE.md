# Legacy formative-listening artifacts

The files in this directory are retained only to preserve the history of the repository. They are excluded from the current manuscript and its evidence package.

The formative exercise did not have prospective institutional approval or exemption and did not enforce adult eligibility. No retrospective approval is claimed. Its aggregate tables must not be presented as an ethically governed human-participant result, and participant-level data must not be redistributed or reused for new analysis.

A future listening or live-performance study must start under a prospectively approved protocol or a documented institutional determination that review is not required.
